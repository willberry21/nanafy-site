Privacy

What we hold, and what we do with it.

Last updated DATE — set this when you publish

The short version

People leave memories on Nanafy at the worst and best moments of their lives. We treat that accordingly. We do not sell anything to anyone, we do not run advertising trackers, and there is no analytics script on this site watching you.

A guest gives us three things: the name they type, the words they write or speak, and any photos they add. Nothing else. No account, no email, no phone number. A host gives us an email address so they can get back into their keepsake.

You can ask us to delete any of it, and we will.

This policy covers nanafy.ai and the pages under it — creating a keepsake, adding a memory, viewing a keepsake, and ordering a printed book.

What a guest gives us

If someone scanned a code at a gathering and left a memory, this is the whole of it:

  • The name you type. You choose what to put. It can be a first name, a full name, or how you were known to them.
  • Your memory. Either words you typed, or a recording of your voice made in your phone's browser.
  • Photos, if you add any.
  • A timestamp, so the keepsake can be ordered.

We do not ask a guest for an email address, a phone number, or an account. We do not read your contacts, your camera roll beyond the photo you pick, or your location.

What a host gives us

  • An email address, and either a password or a one-time sign-in link, so only you can get back into your keepsake.
  • The keepsake's details — the occasion, the person's name, dates, a photo, and the questions you want guests asked.
  • A welcome recording, if you record one.
  • Order details if you buy a printed book. Payment is handled by our print partner, not by us — see below.

Nana AI, and what it is shown

Two features send text to an AI model. We would rather say so here than have you find out later.

Suggesting questions. When a host asks Nana to suggest questions, we send the occasion, the person's name, and whatever the host typed about them. No guest memories are involved — at that point there aren't any.

Arranging the book. If a host asks Nana to arrange the printed book, we send, for each memory: the contributor's name, the question it answered, and the first 240 characters of what they wrote. Recordings and photos are never sent — only text. This only happens when a host presses the button, and the host reviews the result before anything is saved.

Recordings and photos are stored at unlisted web addresses

This is the one thing on this page we would most want you to read.

A keepsake page itself is private — it is reachable only by its code or link, and it is not listed or indexed anywhere. But the recordings and photos inside it are each stored at their own long, unguessable web address, and anyone who has one of those addresses can open that file without signing in. Addresses are random and are never published, so this is not a door standing open — but it is not a lock either.

In practice: do not forward the direct link to a recording to anyone you would not have handed the recording to.

What we deliberately don't do

  • No advertising, and no ad trackers. There is no Facebook pixel, no Google Ads tag, no third-party cookie on this site.
  • No analytics. We are not currently measuring your visit at all. If that ever changes we will name the tool here first.
  • We do not sell or rent anything about you, and we do not share memories with anyone beyond the services listed above.
  • We do not read your keepsake for our own purposes. We may look at a specific keepsake if you ask us for help with it, or if we are legally required to.

How long we keep it

A keepsake is meant to outlast the occasion, so we keep it until someone asks us not to. We do not run a countdown, and we do not delete memories because a host stopped paying for something.

Deleting things

  • A guest can delete the memory they left, from the same device and browser they left it on. If that device is gone, email us and we will remove it.
  • A host can delete any memory in their keepsake, or the whole keepsake, from the keepsake page.
  • Anyone can ask us to remove a memory that is about them, or that they left, by writing to PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL.

Deleting a memory removes it from the keepsake and from our storage. A printed book already in someone's hands is, of course, already printed.

Children

Children leave memories at memorials, and we would not want it otherwise. A child can leave a memory the same way anyone else does, and we collect nothing from them beyond the name they type and what they say.

Hosting a keepsake is different: you must be 18 or older to create one and be responsible for it. If you believe we hold something a child provided that you want removed, write to us and we will remove it without asking you to prove anything.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. If you are in the UK, the EU, or a US state with its own privacy law, you may have further rights — including the right to complain to your data protection regulator.

We will not charge you for asking, and we will not make you create an account to ask. Write to PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL.
