This is a demonstration, not a real family's keepsake. Marion Hale is someone we made up. We wrote these memories, and the four spoken ones are read by a computer so you can hear how a voice memory plays. A real keepsake sounds like the actual people who loved them.

In memory of

Marion Hale

1939 – 2026 · Gathered at her memorial, in one afternoon

41 memories · 12 of them spoken

Paul Hale · her son

0:14

What did she always say?

“Nothing good happens to a closed door.” She meant it about the house and she meant it about people. She would not let you sulk in a bedroom.

June Alvarez · her friend from the choir

Carmen Ruiz · her neighbor of 31 years

0:17

What would you thank her for?

She paid my community college registration in 1994 and told me it was a loan. She never once asked me for it. I found out at the reception that she did the same thing for two other people in this room.

Denise Okafor · her former neighbor

Tessa Hale · her granddaughter

0:10

Where were you happiest together?

The back step, shelling peas, both of us not talking. She was the only person I have ever been able to be quiet around.

Ellen Hale-Boyd · her daughter

Ray Whitcomb · her brother-in-law

0:17

What is something only you know about her?

She kept a running list of every book she ever lent out and who had it. Forty years of it, in the same notebook. Two are still missing and she knew exactly whose houses they were in.

Marcus Hale · her son

What did she teach you?

That you write the thank-you note the same day, and you write it by hand, and you say what the thing actually meant to you — not just thank you for the thing.

Priya Raman · her hospice nurse
